Example scenarios

Always the same pattern: the business describes, the team works together, costs stay within limits, and the change ships without a release queue.

Example scenario · Insurance

"Our core system ships two releases a year."

The claims team wants to handle a new claim type faster. Claims handlers and the team lead describe the process in a guided interview; handlers propose changes, the lead approves. The new flow runs alongside the core system, which stays untouched. If the assessor service changes later or the AI provider goes down, rules and data stay; only the connection is switched.

Together in one workspace No lock-in Costs within limits Your data stays yours

Example scenario · Property management

"Every small change means a quote from our software vendor."

Defect reports arrive by email, phone and paper notes. Property managers, accounting and contractor dispatch agree on report types and who decides. AI routes the reports; people decide. The team adds new report types itself; if a contractor closes down, only the routing changes.

Together in one workspace Costs within limits No lock-in

Example scenario · Plant engineering

"When Mr M. is on holiday, no quote goes out."

Quotes with engineering content depend on a few people. Sales, engineering and installation planning capture rules and experience together. AI drafts the quote and shows what it relies on; engineering reviews and approves. If a supplier discontinues a material, the alternative is recorded once and applies to all future drafts.

Together in one workspace Costs within limits Your data stays yours
